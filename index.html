How Cre8 Studio Works With TikTok

  1. Connect your TikTok account: You click “Connect TikTok” inside Cre8 Studio and are redirected to TikTok’s official authorization page.
  2. Grant permission: TikTok asks you to confirm that Cre8 Studio can upload videos and read basic account information for posting.
  3. Return to Cre8 Studio: After you approve, TikTok redirects you back to our callback page at https://thecre8studio.com/callback.html.
  4. Schedule & auto-post: You choose which videos to publish, set captions/hashtags, and Cre8 Studio sends them to TikTok using the official API.
  5. Full control: You can stop using Cre8 Studio at any time, and you can revoke access from your TikTok account settings.

Cre8 Studio does not sell TikTok data. We only use the permissions shown on the TikTok consent screen to help you upload and manage your own content. For more details, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

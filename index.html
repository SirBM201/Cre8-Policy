Cre8 Studio · Smart video creation & auto-posting

Create once. Publish everywhere.

Cre8 Studio is a creator tool that helps users create, schedule and publish videos to their own channels on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and other platforms. All connections are made through the platforms’ official APIs and secure OAuth – no passwords are ever shared with us.

YouTube: upload & manage videos on your own channel
Meta: schedule posts to Facebook Pages & Instagram
TikTok: short-form clips and re-posts (where available)
Creator dashboard (coming soon)
Built for policy-compliant automations

Cre8 Studio never sells YouTube or social-platform data, and it does not use API-returned data for advertising, social listening, or resale. All access is limited to the features visible inside the user’s dashboard.

What creators can do with Cre8 Studio

1. Connect your own accounts

Creators connect their YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and other accounts directly through each platform’s OAuth or app-linking flow.

  • No passwords are collected by Cre8 Studio.
  • Creators can revoke access at any time from their platform settings.
  • Only the minimum scopes needed for uploads and basic analytics are requested.

2. Upload & schedule videos

Once connected, users can upload ready-made videos or use AI-assisted templates to generate short clips and longer episodes.

  • Publish directly to the creator’s own channel or page.
  • Edit titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails before posting.
  • Schedule posts to go live at optimal times.

3. Multi-platform automation

Creators can send the same video to several platforms at once. For each destination, Cre8 Studio uses that platform’s official API.

  • Per-platform formatting (aspect ratio, captions, hashtags).
  • Respect for each platform’s rate limits and quota rules.
  • Automatic error reporting in the dashboard if a post fails.

4. Performance overview only

For YouTube and other platforms, Cre8 Studio can display high-level performance metrics to the creator.

  • Examples: views, likes, watch time, impressions.
  • Data is shown only to the owner of the connected account.
  • No public reports, resale or third-party analytics are generated.
Information for YouTube & platform reviewers

How Cre8 Studio uses platform APIs

Cre8 Studio acts purely as a creator productivity tool. It does not provide social listening, competitive intelligence or audience-surveillance features.

  • Authentication: Users sign in with OAuth for each platform. Tokens are stored securely and are only used to perform user-initiated or user-configured actions.
  • YouTube Data: Used only to upload and manage the user’s own videos and to show simple performance dashboards. No data is sold, shared or combined with external datasets.
  • Other Platforms: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and others are used in a similar way – strictly for posting and basic performance views for the account owner.
  • Revocation: Users can disconnect any integration from the Cre8 Studio dashboard or directly via the platform (for YouTube, at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

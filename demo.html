Cre8 Studio Posting Engine

This page explains how the Cre8 Studio Posting Engine interacts with TikTok when a user authorizes our tool.

1. What this tool does

Cre8 Studio Posting Engine is a scheduling and publishing assistant for short-form video content. After a creator connects their own TikTok account and grants permission through the official TikTok OAuth flow, the tool can:

  • Upload videos that the creator has selected and provided to the tool
  • Attach captions, hashtags and basic metadata chosen by the creator
  • Schedule or immediately publish those videos to the creator’s TikTok account
  • Retrieve basic posting status (for example, whether an upload succeeded)

All actions are performed only after the user has explicitly granted permission via TikTok’s official authorization screen.

2. How TikTok data is used

When a user connects TikTok, the Service receives an access token and the minimum account information required by the selected scope. This is used solely to:

  • Authenticate the user with TikTok
  • Publish user-approved videos on their behalf
  • Read basic responses from TikTok about the upload (such as success or failure)

We do not use TikTok data for profiling, advertising, resale, or sharing with third parties. We do not request or access data that is unrelated to publishing content and checking posting status.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not scrape TikTok or collect data outside the official API.
  • We do not download or repost other users’ TikTok content without permission.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade TikTok user data.
  • We do not perform hidden or background actions on a user’s account.
  • We do not bypass TikTok’s rules, limits, or community guidelines.

All usage is intended to remain fully compliant with TikTok’s Platform and Developer Terms.

4. Security and data retention

Tokens and identifiers received from TikTok are stored only to the extent needed to provide the Service functions described above. Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting Cre8 Studio from within TikTok or by contacting us.

We use HTTPS for all public pages, including this one, and follow standard security practices appropriate for a small posting tool. Detailed information can be found in our Privacy Policy and Platform Policy.

5. How to revoke access

A user can stop Cre8 Studio from accessing their TikTok account at any time by:

  • Removing the Cre8 Studio authorization from their TikTok account settings; or
  • Contacting us and requesting that we remove their tokens and disconnect access.

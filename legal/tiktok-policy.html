TikTok Posting & Transformation Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Purpose of This Policy

This page explains how Cre8 Studio interacts with TikTok and sets clear rules for using our tools with TikTok accounts. It is intended to provide transparency to users, TikTok reviewers, and partners about what our system does and does not do.

2. What Cre8 Studio Does with TikTok

When a user connects their TikTok account and grants permission, Cre8 Studio may:

  • Upload and publish videos to TikTok on the user’s behalf.
  • Attach titles, captions, tags, and other metadata configured by the user or generated by our AI tools.
  • Optionally read performance metrics (for example, views or likes on the user’s own posts) where TikTok’s APIs and user permissions allow this.

Cre8 Studio does not:

  • Buy or sell followers, likes, or views.
  • Manipulate engagement metrics or ranking algorithms.
  • Perform any actions on other users’ content (such as auto-liking or auto-commenting).

3. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for ensuring that:

  • They comply with TikTok’s Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and Advertising Policies at all times.
  • They have all rights and permissions needed for any content published via Cre8 Studio, including rights to use any third-party audio, video, or imagery.
  • They set appropriate posting schedules and do not use Cre8 Studio to spam TikTok or violate rate limits or automation restrictions.

4. AI Transformation & Source Material

Cre8 Studio can transform content using AI (for example, adding overlays, commentary, review formats, reaction videos, or rebranding). These tools are designed to help creators add value and context to their content. However:

  • Users must ensure that any source clips (such as movie scenes, trailers, or sports clips) are used lawfully, including under fair use or equivalent rules where applicable.
  • Users must follow TikTok rules on copyrighted material, synthetic media, and use of third parties’ likenesses.
  • Cre8 Studio may provide guidelines and warnings, but creators remain legally responsible for how they use third-party material.

5. Prohibited Content on TikTok via Cre8 Studio

It is strictly prohibited to use Cre8 Studio to publish content to TikTok that includes or promotes:

  • Hate speech, harassment, or targeted attacks on individuals or groups.
  • Graphic violence, self-harm content, or sexually explicit material that violates TikTok rules.
  • Deceptive or misleading claims, scams, or impersonation of real people or brands.
  • Unauthorized distribution of full movies, long untransformed sports broadcasts, or other clearly infringing material.
  • Any activity designed only to manipulate metrics (views, likes, followers) instead of providing genuine content.

6. Synthetic Media, Avatars & Voice

Cre8 Studio may integrate with AI avatar and voice tools. Where synthetic media is used in videos posted to TikTok:

  • Users must follow TikTok’s rules on synthetic or manipulated media, including any required disclosures.
  • Users must not impersonate real people without clear permission and transparent labeling.
  • We may offer tools or labels to help indicate when content includes AI-generated visuals or speech, but users are responsible for using them correctly.

7. Automation Limits

Cre8 Studio focuses on scheduling and posting content. We intentionally limit our tools to respect TikTok’s automation and anti-spam policies. In particular:

  • We only perform posting actions that users have explicitly authorized and configured in the platform.
  • We do not run automated like, follow, comment, or view generation systems.
  • We monitor system usage and may pause or restrict accounts that appear to be abusing the Service or violating TikTok rules.

8. Data & Permissions

When a user connects their TikTok account, we store only the minimal tokens and identifiers needed to:

  • Upload videos and metadata according to the user’s configuration.
  • Optionally retrieve performance metrics for that user’s own content, if they have granted such permission.

Additional details on how we handle data are available in our Privacy Policy.

9. Policy Enforcement

If we learn that a user is using Cre8 Studio to violate TikTok policies, applicable law, or this TikTok Posting & Transformation Policy, we may:

  • Warn the user and request corrective action.
  • Temporarily suspend TikTok posting through our system.
  • Terminate the user’s access to Cre8 Studio where necessary.

1. Overview

Cre8 Studio respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects our users to do the same. This policy explains how we address claims that content posted via Cre8 Studio infringes copyrights or other intellectual property rights.

2. User Responsibility for Content

Users are responsible for ensuring that any content they upload to or publish through Cre8 Studio does not infringe the rights of others. This includes:

  • Videos, audio, and images.
  • Text such as titles, descriptions, and overlays.
  • Any third-party material used in AI transformations or review formats.

3. Submitting a Takedown Request

If you believe that content published via Cre8 Studio infringes your copyright or other intellectual property rights, please send us a notice that includes:

  • Your full name and contact details (email and, if possible, postal address).
  • A clear description of the work you believe has been infringed, and evidence that you are the rights holder or authorized to act on their behalf.
  • A description of the specific content you believe is infringing, including any relevant links or identifiers (for example, a TikTok link or Cre8 Studio reference).
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, their agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Please send your notice to:

4. Our Response

When we receive a complete and reasonable notice, we may take steps such as:

  • Reviewing the content identified in the request.
  • Temporarily limiting or disabling access to the content.
  • Notifying the relevant user about the claim and requesting more information.
  • Removing or restricting content where we reasonably believe infringement has occurred or where we are legally required to do so.

5. Repeat or Serious Violations

We may suspend or terminate access to Cre8 Studio for users who repeatedly infringe rights or who engage in serious violations of this policy.

6. No Legal Advice

Nothing on this page is legal advice. If you are unsure about your rights or obligations, you should consult a qualified legal professional.

Privacy Policy – Cre8 Studio

Last updated: December 2025

Cre8 Studio ("Cre8 Studio", "the Platform", "we", "our", or "us") provides automated video creation, rebranding, and publishing services across multiple social platforms, including YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and others that may be added from time to time (collectively, "Connected Platforms"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use Cre8 Studio or connect your social accounts.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account and OAuth Authorization Data

When you connect your YouTube, Facebook/Instagram, TikTok, or other social accounts to Cre8 Studio, we receive information through official OAuth flows, including:

  • Access tokens and refresh tokens issued by the Connected Platforms
  • Channel IDs, page IDs, user IDs, and basic profile/channel/page information
  • The specific permissions (scopes) you grant to Cre8 Studio

We never collect or store your passwords.

b. Content You Provide

We collect content and metadata that you choose to upload or manage through Cre8 Studio, such as:

  • Video files, audio files, and thumbnails
  • Titles, descriptions, tags, hashtags, and scheduled posting times
  • Branding overlays, templates, and configuration settings

c. Usage and Technical Data

We may collect limited technical and usage data, such as:

  • Log entries for publishing actions and automation jobs
  • IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and error diagnostics
  • Aggregate statistics about feature usage to improve service reliability

2. How We Use Your Information

Cre8 Studio uses the information we collect only for the purposes you authorize, including:

  • Connecting and authenticating your accounts with Connected Platforms
  • Uploading and publishing videos to the channels, pages, or profiles you select
  • Running automations and schedules that you explicitly configure
  • Displaying connection status and basic channel/page information in your dashboard
  • Providing customer support and troubleshooting technical issues

We do not use YouTube Data or other platform data for advertising, resale, data brokering, or unrelated analytics.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or YouTube Data. We only share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Connected Platforms, strictly as required to perform actions you request, such as publishing a video or updating metadata.
  • With payment processors (for example, card processors or online payment gateways) to process your subscription fees or other authorized payments.
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process, and only to the extent legally necessary.

4. User Control, Revocation, and Data Deletion

You can disconnect any Connected Platform from Cre8 Studio at any time from within your account settings. When you disconnect:

  • OAuth tokens and related credentials are deleted from our systems.
  • Cre8 Studio immediately loses access to your accounts on that platform.
  • Automated posting, scheduling, and content management for that platform are disabled until you reconnect.

If you permanently delete your Cre8 Studio account or submit a valid data deletion request, we will remove your stored tokens and associated profile data, subject to any legal retention obligations.

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including encrypted storage of tokens where applicable and access controls limited to essential personnel and systems. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as it is needed to provide Cre8 Studio services, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, or comply with legal obligations. OAuth tokens and related authorization data are kept only while your account remains connected.

7. Compliance with Platform Policies

Cre8 Studio is designed to comply with:

  • YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Platform Terms and Developer Policies
  • TikTok Developer Terms and any related platform policies

We access and use data from these platforms only within the scope of permissions granted by the user and only for the functions described in this Policy and in our Terms of Service.

8. Children’s Privacy

Cre8 Studio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have unintentionally collected such information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through in-app notifications or email.

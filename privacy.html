Privacy Policy – Cre8 Studio Posting Engine

Last updated: 28 November 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how BMS Creative Concept ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Cre8 Studio Posting Engine tool ("the Service").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Basic account details such as your name or username;
  • Contact information you choose to share, such as email address;
  • Content you upload for posting (videos, captions, hashtags, thumbnails);
  • Settings and preferences related to scheduling or auto-posting.

1.2 Technical Credentials

To connect to TikTok and other platforms, we may store:

  • API keys or access tokens generated via official platform APIs;
  • Account IDs or page IDs needed to post on your behalf.

These credentials are used only to operate the Service (for example, to publish videos and retrieve basic posting status).

1.3 Usage Data

We may collect basic technical information about how you use the Service, such as:

  • Logs of posts made via the Service (time, destination, basic status);
  • Device or browser type, and general technical logs for debugging.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Connect your account to TikTok and other platforms;
  • Schedule and publish content on your behalf;
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • Monitor for abuse, security issues, and technical problems;
  • Communicate with you about updates, support, or important changes.

We process your information based on your consent and/or our legitimate interest in providing and improving the Service. By using the Service, you consent to this processing.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as is reasonably necessary to operate the Service, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your account-related data by contacting us.

6. Security

7. Your Rights

Depending on your country, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal requirements;
  • Withdraw your consent to processing where applicable.

8. Third-Party Platforms

When you connect TikTok or other platforms, their own privacy policies and terms apply to how they handle your data. We do not control how TikTok or any other platform processes information once it leaves our system.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

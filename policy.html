Cre8 Studio – Platform & API Use Policy

Last updated: December 2025

This Platform & API Use Policy ("Policy") describes how Cre8 Studio interacts with third-party platforms such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and others, and sets out rules for how creators may use these integrations through Cre8 Studio.

1. Purpose of Integrations

Cre8 Studio integrations are designed solely to help creators:

  • Create, rebrand, and prepare video content;
  • Upload and publish content to their own channels, pages, or profiles;
  • Schedule posts and automate repetitive publishing workflows;
  • Manage metadata such as titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

When you connect a YouTube channel, Cre8 Studio uses YouTube API Services to:

  • Authenticate your account via OAuth 2.0;
  • Upload videos and set or update titles, descriptions, tags, and privacy status;
  • Retrieve basic channel information required to display connection status.

Cre8 Studio does not use YouTube Data for analytics, ad targeting, resale, or any secondary use outside of the publishing workflow you create. Your use of Cre8 Studio with YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service, as well as the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Use of Meta (Facebook & Instagram) APIs

When you connect Facebook pages or Instagram business accounts, Cre8 Studio uses Meta APIs to:

  • Authenticate you via Meta’s OAuth flow;
  • Upload reels, videos, and associated metadata to the pages/accounts you select;
  • Read back limited status information (for example, whether a post was successfully created).

Your use of these features must comply with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Cre8 Studio does not share Meta data with unrelated third parties.

4. Use of TikTok and Other Platforms

For TikTok and other integrated platforms, Cre8 Studio uses only the official APIs and OAuth flows provided by each platform. Data is accessed and used solely to:

  • Authenticate your account;
  • Upload videos/shorts and set metadata;
  • Perform other actions that you explicitly initiate or schedule.

You must comply with each platform’s terms of service and community guidelines when publishing content through Cre8 Studio.

5. Prohibited Uses

You may not use Cre8 Studio or its integrations to:

  • Collect or attempt to collect data about other users without their consent;
  • Bypass or interfere with any platform’s security, rate limits, or access controls;
  • Manipulate metrics (views, likes, followers) in ways that violate platform rules;
  • Engage in spam, harassment, misinformation, or illegal activities;
  • Build a competing data set or analytics product based primarily on YouTube Data or other platform data obtained through Cre8 Studio.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Cre8 Studio stores OAuth tokens and minimal profile/channel information only as long as needed to provide publishing and automation services. Tokens are deleted when you disconnect an account or when they expire and are not renewed.

7. User Control and Revocation

You can revoke Cre8 Studio’s access to any platform at any time by:

  • Disconnecting the account from within the Cre8 Studio dashboard; and/or
  • Using the platform’s own security page (for example, Google security settings for YouTube).

Once revoked, Cre8 Studio can no longer call the APIs or act on your behalf for that platform.

8. Reporting Abuse

If you believe a Cre8 Studio user is abusing integrations or violating this Policy, please contact us with details so we can investigate and take appropriate action, which may include suspension or termination of access.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Platform & API Use Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our integrations or in third-party platform requirements. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
